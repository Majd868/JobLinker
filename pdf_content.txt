JobLinker is a full-featured Android application designed to connect employers
and job seekers on a single platform. During registration, each user selects their
account type:
• Employer: can post jobs and manage applicants.
• Job Seeker: can browse job listings, apply filters, and submit applications.
Main Features:
• Post, manage, and filter job listings easily.
• Advanced search based on job type, category, salary, and location.
• Real-time one-to-one chat using Firebase Firestore.
• Live voice calls powered by Agora RTC SDK.
• Push notifications via Firebase Cloud Messaging (FCM).
• Complete user profiles including avatar, skills, and biography.
• Multi-language and multi-currency support.
• Email and phone OTP verification using Firebase Authentication.
